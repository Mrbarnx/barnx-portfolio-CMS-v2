# Dispatch Now — marketing demo video

## Purpose

Show how a focused landing page can move campaign traffic from one clear delivery offer into a structured lead request. This is an independently created case study, not a claimed client result.

## Master cut — 60–75 seconds

| Time | Story | Visual direction | Narration / caption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7s | Problem hook | Social ad → generic homepage → open chat | “A strong offer can still lose attention when campaign traffic has no focused next step.” |
| 7–15s | Existing friction | Questions about coverage, cutoff time and package details | “The visitor may need to ask what the offer covers, whether they qualify and what to send.” |
| 15–25s | Introduce solution | Reveal the Dispatch Now hero and primary action | “Dispatch Now keeps one same-day delivery offer, its conditions and its action in one journey.” |
| 25–45s | Product proof | Scroll offer context and FAQs; complete the lead form | “The page explains the service, answers the main objections and collects the details needed for follow-up.” |
| 45–55s | Handoff | Show review, confirmation and structured request | “Before submission, the customer can review the request. The business receives a clearer lead.” |
| 55–65s | Outcome | Campaign → focused page → structured lead → follow-up | “The system is designed to reduce uncertainty between an advert and the first sales response.” |
| 65–75s | CTA | Barnx end card and project link | “Is your campaign sending people into an unclear enquiry process? Let’s design a better path.” |

## Required captures

- Desktop hero and offer context
- Mobile landing-page scroll
- FAQ interaction
- Form validation
- Completed request review and confirmation
- Business-problem workflow graphic

## Export set

- 16:9 master, 1080p, 60–75 seconds
- 9:16 social cut using the same narrative
- 20–30 second outreach cut: problem, proof moment, CTA
- Burned-caption version plus an `.srt` file
- Poster frame for the project detail page

## Evidence boundary

Do not claim measured conversion gains, delivery performance or client revenue. Describe the page as designed to focus the campaign journey and demonstrate a completed lead flow.
