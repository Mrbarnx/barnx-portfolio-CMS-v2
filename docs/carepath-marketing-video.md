# CarePath marketing demo — production brief

## Purpose

Turn the CarePath public build into a reusable 60–75 second sales asset for social content and conversations with appointment-based businesses.

The video sells the business problem and the demonstrated workflow. It must not imply CarePath is a real clinic, that a client commissioned it, or that it produced measured booking improvements.

## Core message

Appointment requests handled through open-ended messages may require several rounds of questions. A guided website can collect the service, practitioner preference, suitable time and contact information in one reviewable request.

## Timeline

| Time | Story | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 sec | Hook: “How many messages does it take to request one clinic appointment?” | Short message bubbles: service? doctor? day? time? |
| 7–15 sec | Show the friction: information arrives in pieces and still needs clarification. | Scattered message details converge into an incomplete request. |
| 15–24 sec | Introduce CarePath as a concept solution. | Homepage hero and “Request an appointment” action. |
| 24–48 sec | Demonstrate the working flow. | Select service → practitioner → sample date/period → contact consent → review. |
| 48–59 sec | Show the structured handoff. | Demo confirmation reference and one structured request card. |
| 59–68 sec | Outcome: clearer information before human follow-up. | Workflow: visitor → guided request → clinic review → follow-up. |
| 68–75 sec | CTA. | “Does your business handle bookings manually? Request a solution.” |

## Narration draft

“How many messages does it take to request one clinic appointment? When bookings begin in an open chat, the service, practitioner, suitable day and contact details can arrive one question at a time. CarePath is a public concept showing a clearer first step. A visitor chooses the service, adds a practitioner preference, selects a suitable period, provides contact details and reviews everything before submitting. The demonstration does not diagnose patients or confirm a real appointment. It shows how one guided flow can prepare a structured request for human follow-up. Does your business handle bookings manually? Request a solution from Barnx.”

## Capture list

1. Homepage hero and services.
2. Open the appointment modal.
3. Select “General consultation.”
4. Choose “No preference.”
5. Select one sample day and period.
6. Enter clearly fictional contact information.
7. Show the review screen slowly enough to read.
8. Submit and show the demo reference.
9. End on the Barnx CTA card.

## Production rules

- Use a vertical 1080 × 1920 master; retain a clean 16:9 crop for LinkedIn and portfolio use.
- Keep captions inside safe margins.
- Mix interface capture, captions and one simple workflow animation.
- Do not show real patient information.
- Do not claim conversion, revenue, time-saving or client results.
- Publish a Watch Demo link only after the final video exists and has been checked.
