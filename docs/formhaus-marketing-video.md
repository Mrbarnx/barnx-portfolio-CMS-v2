# Formhaus marketing demo

Classification: Independent Case Study · Web Design & Development · Product Catalogue
Target runtime: 70–75 seconds
Shareable page: `/projects/formhaus-furniture-catalogue/demo`

## Core story

Furniture buyers may have to ask for photos, materials, dimensions and availability across several messages before they can narrow down suitable products. Formhaus demonstrates a visual catalogue that lets a buyer explore, compare and prepare a more focused enquiry before speaking with sales.

## Recording data

Use only the fictional products already present in the experience. Do not introduce real customer names, prices, stock claims, supplier relationships or sales results.

## Capture checklist

- Record desktop product browsing at 1440 × 900 or larger.
- Capture a separate mobile pass for the vertical version; do not crop the desktop recording blindly.
- Keep the search, filters, product detail and shortlist interactions readable.
- Show the final enquiry summary long enough to understand the business handoff.
- Label any sample message graphic as an example workflow.
- Export 16:9 master, 9:16 social cut, SRT captions and poster frame.
- Verify that no browser bookmarks, private accounts, tokens or unrelated tabs appear.

## Evidence boundary

The video may state that the catalogue, filtering, detail, shortlist and enquiry-preparation flow work. It must not claim increased conversions, reduced support time, live stock accuracy or client results unless those are later verified.
